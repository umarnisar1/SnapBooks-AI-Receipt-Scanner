2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: Email address, display name, and profile photo when you sign in via Google or other providers
  • Receipt Images and Files: Images or PDFs you capture, upload, or batch import for scanning
  • Expense Data: Vendor names, amounts, dates, categories, currencies, taxes, line items, notes, receipt status, and duplicate/review signals
  • Receipt Protection Data: Optional return deadlines, warranty expiry dates, reminder lead times, reminder notification identifiers, and notes you add to protect a purchase
  • Reports and Exports: Tax report summaries, CSV/PDF export contents, and related file metadata generated from your saved expense data
  • Communications: Messages you send to us via email or in-app support

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device type, OS version, unique device identifiers (Android Advertising ID, Apple IDFA)
  • Usage Data: Features used, scan counts, batch import actions, report/export actions, reminder scheduling status, session duration, crash reports, and error logs
  • Log Data: IP address, access times, and screens viewed
  • Analytics: Aggregated usage metrics via Google Firebase Analytics

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Google Sign-In: Name, email, and profile picture (only what you authorise)
  • RevenueCat: Subscription status and purchase history (payment details held by App Stores)
  • Google AdMob: Advertising identifiers for ad serving

2.4 Guest Users: Identified by an anonymous device fingerprint. No name or email is collected.

6. Receipt Image, PDF & AI Processing

When you scan or import a receipt:

  1. The image or PDF is captured, selected, or queued on-device
  2. It is transmitted via HTTPS to our backend server
  3. Our server forwards the file to an AI provider for OCR extraction
  4. Structured data, including receipt fields, categories, tax values, and line items, is returned and stored in your account or guest storage
  5. AI providers process the file under their own retention policies

Important: AI-extracted data may be inaccurate. You must verify all extracted data before use. SnapBooks accepts no liability for errors in AI-generated data.

Reports and exports: PDF and CSV reports are generated from your saved expense records and are shared only when you choose to share or export them.

Return and warranty reminders: Purchase protection dates and notes are stored with the expense. Reminder notifications are scheduled through the device notification system and can be removed by editing or deleting protection details.

We recommend not scanning receipts containing sensitive personal information beyond what is needed for expense tracking.

8. Advertising ID & Device Identifiers

SnapBooks and its advertising partner Google AdMob use the Android Advertising ID (GAID) and/or Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) to:

  • Serve personalised advertisements based on your interests
  • Measure ad performance and attribution
  • Detect and prevent invalid ad traffic and fraud
  • Generate aggregated analytics and reporting

How to opt out of personalised ads:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset Advertising ID or select "Opt out of Ads Personalisation"
  • iOS 14+: Settings → Privacy → Tracking → disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"

Opting out means you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised based on your interests. Certain data may still be collected for fraud prevention and ad measurement purposes.

10. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as: your account is active; it is necessary to provide the Service; required by applicable law; or necessary to resolve disputes or enforce agreements.

Retention periods by data type:

  • Account data (name, email): Retained until account deletion, then deleted within 30 days
  • Receipt images, PDFs, expense data, line items, and purchase protection data: Retained while account is active or until you delete them; deleted within 30 days of account deletion
  • Generated reports and exports: Created from saved expense data and retained only as long as needed to generate, cache, or share the file unless you save it externally
  • Usage & analytics data: Retained for up to 24 months in aggregated or anonymised form
  • Log & crash data: Retained for up to 90 days
  • Advertising data: Governed by Google AdMob's own retention policies

Anonymised, aggregated data that cannot identify you may be retained indefinitely for analytics and product improvement.
